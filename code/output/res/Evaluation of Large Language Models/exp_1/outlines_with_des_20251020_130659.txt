### 2.1 Core Natural Language Processing Abilities
Description: Evaluating the fundamental capabilities of large language models in conventional natural language processing tasks such as translation, summarization, and sentiment analysis. This subsection emphasizes the importance of assessing performance on these core tasks to determine the model's proficiency and applicability.
1. Translation: Evaluating the accuracy and fluency of translations across different languages, encompassing challenges such as idiomatic expressions and contextual understanding.
2. Summarization: Assessing the model's ability to generate concise and coherent summaries while preserving the essential information and context of the original text.
3. Sentiment Analysis: Measuring the effectiveness of models in identifying and categorizing emotions in text, including handling nuanced and ambiguous sentiment.

### 2.2 Reasoning and Comprehension Abilities
Description: Investigating the logical reasoning and complex comprehension skills of large language models. This subsection focuses on the model's ability to understand, infer, and process complex information logically.
1. Logical Reasoning: Evaluating the model’s proficiency in solving logical problems and puzzles, following structured logic sequences, and producing coherent reasoning outputs.
2. Deductive and Inductive Reasoning: Differentiating between the model's abilities in deductive reasoning, where conclusions are derived from general rules, and inductive reasoning, where generalizations are made from specific instances.
3. Multi-step Reasoning: Assessing the model’s performance in tasks requiring elaborate reasoning processes involving multiple steps to reach a conclusion or solve a problem.

### 2.3 Specialized Domain Performance

2. Financial Analysis: Measuring the model's capabilities in financial forecasting, sentiment analysis of financial news, and comprehending complex financial terminology and data.
3. Legal Reasoning: Assessing the model’s performance in tasks related to legal judgments, drafting legal documents, and understanding legal language and precedence.

2. Transparency: Assessing the transparency of the model's decision-making processes and the readability and interpretability of its outputs.
